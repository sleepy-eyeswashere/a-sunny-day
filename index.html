To My Blueberrypie <3

"here with you"

today is your birthday.
sometimes I wonder how one life can quietly shift the orbit of another without ever knowing it has done so.

before you, my days simply passed.
after you, they learned how to wait—
for your voice,
for your laughter,
for those ordinary moments that only became unforgettable because you were there.

you didn't come into my life like a storm.
you came like light slipping through the edge of a window into a dark room—
so gently that I never realised the exact moment darkness stopped feeling the way it used to.
or perhaps
you were the warmth that slowly returns to frozen hands;
so softly that I never noticed the precise moment pain surrendered to peace.

maybe you were the missing colour
in every sunset
I had ever watched.
the sky had always been beautiful,
but until it borrowed your light,
I never knew beauty itself could be incomplete.

thank you
for becoming the person
beside whom
my heart
has never felt
like a stranger.

and if I were allowed only one wish,
it would be this—that one year,
when your birthday
finds us again,
won't have to love
this day from afar.

I hope I can spend
every hour of it beside you—
watching the morning arrive,
watching the evening fade,
laughing with you,
talking with you,
sharing even
the quiet between us.
and knowing there is nowhere else in the world
I'd rather be.

Happy Birthday, my Blueberrypie.

from your vanilla
